About Hostello 🏠

India's most trusted student hostel discovery and booking platform

🎯 Our Mission

Hostello is dedicated to helping students find comfortable and affordable accommodation in any city. We connect students with verified hostels, making the search and booking process simple, transparent, and secure.

⚙️ How It Works

  1. Search hostels by city or name
  2. Compare details, amenities, and pricing
  3. Select your check-in and check-out dates
  4. Pay securely via Card, UPI, or QR Code
  5. Receive instant booking confirmation

✨ Key Features

  • 10+ cities across India
  • Verified hostel listings
  • Multiple secure payment options
  • Student-friendly pricing
  • Real-time availability
  • Admin dashboard for full control

👨‍💻 About the Creator

Hostello was built to solve the real problem students face when relocating to new cities for education. Our platform makes hostel hunting stress-free, efficient, and reliable — so students can focus on what matters most: their studies.
